# Markdown Notes

## Symbols and Meanings
- Headings: # through #####
- Bold: **text** or __text__
> **text** or __text__
- Italic: *text* or _text_
- Bold and Italic: ***text***
- Block Quote: > text
- > Quoted Text
- Ordered List: 1. 2. 3. ...
  1. First Thing
  2. Second Thing
  3. And So On
- Unordered List: -, *, or +
  + Another Thing
  * Yet Another Thing
- Inline Code: backtics `code`
- Code Block: triple backticks ```code```
- Horizontal Rule: ---, ***, ___
---
***
___
- Link: [text](url)
- Image: ![alt](url)
